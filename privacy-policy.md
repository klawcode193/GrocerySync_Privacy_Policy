2. Data We Collect or Access
GrocerySync is designed to minimize personal data collection. We do not operate our own cloud servers for storing your grocery data.
The app may collect or access the following categories of information:
•	App content you create: shopping lists, grocery items, categories, recipes, notes, settings, and related app content.
•	Google account information: if you choose to sign in with Google, GrocerySync may receive your Google account name, email address, and authentication tokens from Google’s OAuth service.
•	Google Drive files created or used by GrocerySync: if you enable Google Drive sync, GrocerySync stores its primary sync file in your Google Drive app data folder. This app data folder is managed by Google Drive and is generally not visible in your normal Drive file list. If you use shared list features, GrocerySync may also create separate visible Google Drive files for shared lists.
•	Device and app preferences: theme, display settings, purchase state, sync settings, and similar preferences stored locally on your device.
•	Advertising identifiers and ad-related data: if you use the free, ad-supported version, Google Mobile Ads may collect device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interaction data, and other information as described in Google’s privacy policies and advertising policies.
•	Purchase-related status: if you purchase an ad-free upgrade, the app may store purchase status locally and may rely on the app store provider to process and validate purchases.
We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information such as government identifiers, financial account numbers, health data, precise location, or biometric information.
3. How We Use Information
GrocerySync uses information only for the following purposes:
•	To operate the app and provide grocery list, recipe, category, and settings features.
•	To sync your data across your devices if you choose to enable Google Drive sync.
•	To support optional shared lists if you choose to create or join shared lists.
•	To remember your preferences and purchase status.
•	To show ads in the free version of the app.
•	To maintain app security, troubleshoot issues, and prevent misuse.
We do not sell your grocery content. We do not use your grocery content for advertising profiling. We do not use Google Drive data or Google account data to train AI models.
4. Google Sign-In and Google Drive Sync
Google Sign-In and Google Drive sync are optional. You may use GrocerySync without enabling Google sync, although cross-device sync requires signing in with Google.
If you enable Google sync:
•	GrocerySync uses Google OAuth to access your Google account only for the permissions you approve.
•	GrocerySync stores the main sync file in your Google Drive app data folder.
•	GrocerySync reads and writes only the files needed to provide sync and shared list functionality.
•	GrocerySync does not upload your grocery data to our own servers.
•	You can revoke GrocerySync’s Google access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.
•	You can delete GrocerySync data from your device by deleting app data or uninstalling the app.
•	You can delete synced app data by using Google’s app data management tools or by revoking access and deleting related GrocerySync files where visible.
GrocerySync’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide or improve user-facing app features, comply with applicable law, protect against abuse or security threats, or as part of a user-directed action such as sharing a list.
5. Shared Lists
If you choose to share a list, GrocerySync may create a separate Google Drive file for that shared list and configure Google Drive sharing permissions.
Depending on the sharing option used, anyone with the sharing link may be able to view or edit the shared list file. You are responsible for deciding who receives the sharing link.
You can stop sharing by deleting the shared file or removing its Google Drive sharing permissions. Shared list recipients may retain copies or information they already accessed.
6. Advertising
The free version of GrocerySync may display ads through Google Mobile Ads or related Google advertising services.
Google’s advertising services may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. This data is handled according to Google’s privacy policy and advertising policies.
You can limit ad tracking or personalized ads through your device settings, Google account ad settings, or by purchasing the ad-free upgrade if available.
We do not provide your grocery list content to advertising networks.

9. Data Storage and Retention
App data is stored locally on your device unless you enable Google Drive sync.
If Google Drive sync is enabled, the main GrocerySync sync file is stored in your Google Drive app data folder. Shared lists may be stored as separate visible Google Drive files.
We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the app functionality you use:
•	Local data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app.
•	Google Drive sync data remains in your Google account until you delete it, revoke access, or use Google’s tools to remove app data.
•	Shared list files remain in Google Drive until deleted by the file owner or until sharing permissions are removed.
•	Purchase records may be retained by the applicable app store according to that store’s policies.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
