# GrocerySync Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 28, 2026**

This is the single canonical GrocerySync privacy policy. The in-app policy (Settings > Legal > Privacy Policy), https://grocerysync.online/privacy and the public GrocerySync_Privacy_Policy repository all carry this same text.

GrocerySync ("we", "our", "us") is a free grocery list app for Android, iOS, web, Windows, macOS and Linux. It is supported only by optional donations. GrocerySync shows **no ads**, has **no in-app purchases** and contains **no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs**.

## 1. Who we are
GrocerySync is operated by its independent developer (the data controller). Contact: **support@grocerysync.online**

## 2. What data is handled
- **Your app content** (lists, items, categories, recipes, preferences) is stored on your device. We do not run servers and never receive this content.
- **Optional Google Drive sync.** If you sign in with Google, the app receives your Google account name and email from Google so it can sync. Your content is saved to your own Google Drive (scopes `drive.file` and `drive.appdata`). We do not receive or store it. The main sync file lives in a "GrocerySync" folder that the app creates in your Google Drive. The `drive.appdata` scope is used only to migrate older installs whose sync file was in Drive's hidden app data folder. By default the sync file is plain JSON, so anyone with access to your Google account can read it. Optional passphrase encryption is available in Sync settings.
- **Shared lists.** When you share a list, the app creates a separate Google Drive file for that list in your Drive and turns on link sharing for it. Anyone with the link can view the list, and can also edit it if you choose to allow editing (view-only is the default). You decide who gets the link. Stop sharing by deleting the shared file or removing its sharing permissions in Google Drive. People you shared with may keep what they already saw.
- **Item photos.** Photos you attach to items are stored on your device. They are uploaded to a "photos" folder inside your GrocerySync Drive folder only if you turn on photo sync, which is off by default.
- **Sign-in tokens** are kept on your device in platform secure storage. They are cleared when you sign out or delete your data. We never see your Google password.
- **Donations.** Donations are optional. The in-app Support screen opens our Ko-fi page (ko-fi.com/beam42) in your browser. Ko-fi's privacy policy covers any data you give Ko-fi. The app does not get your payment details.

We do **not** collect analytics, crash reports, usage statistics, advertising identifiers, location or behavioral tracking data.

## 3. Google user data (Limited Use)

GrocerySync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as necessary to provide user-facing features, to comply with applicable law, to protect against abuse or security threats, or as part of an action you direct, such as sharing a list.

## 4. Health data (Washington My Health My Data Act)
GrocerySync **does not collect, share or sell consumer health data**. That includes data inferred from your grocery lists. Your list content never reaches us, and we do not analyze it to infer health status.

## 5. How data is used
Your data is used only to run the app and, if you choose, to sync it across your devices through your Google Drive. We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

## 6. Third parties
- Google Sign-In / Google Drive (optional): https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Ko-fi (optional donations, opened in your browser): https://more.ko-fi.com/privacy

## 7. Retention and deletion
Your data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Synced data stays in your Google Drive until you delete it. You can delete all your data at any time:

- **In the app:** go to **Settings > Sync > Delete my data**. This erases local data, removes the GrocerySync sync file from your Google Drive and signs you out.
- **On the web:** follow the instructions at **https://grocerysync.online/delete-data**, or email support@grocerysync.online.

You can also revoke GrocerySync's access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## 8. Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR, or California law), you may have rights to:

- access your data
- correct it
- delete it
- export it (use Export in the app)
- object to or restrict processing
- withdraw consent (sign out)
- complain to your data protection authority

We do not sell or share personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for using your rights. Contact support@grocerysync.online. We respond within 30 days.

**Legal basis (EEA/UK):** performance of a contract (providing the app and the sync you request) and consent (optional Google sync).

## 9. Do Not Track
We do no cross-site tracking, so GrocerySync behaves the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

## 10. Children
You must be at least 13 years old to use GrocerySync (or older where local law requires, e.g. 16 in parts of the EEA). GrocerySync is intended for a 13+ audience and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact support@grocerysync.online and we will delete it.

## 11. Security
- Content travels to Google over TLS using OAuth 2.0.
- On mobile, local data sits in the app's sandboxed storage.
- On desktop, local data is stored in your OS user profile. It is not encrypted by the app, so use full-disk encryption.
- No system is perfectly secure.

If a data breach happens, we follow our breach response plan and notify affected people as the law requires.

## 12. International transfers
Google may process synced data in the United States and other countries under its approved transfer safeguards.

## 13. Changes
We will update the effective date above and, for material changes, give notice in the app.
